Data lifecycle policy
Learning content

SCORM packages, lesson materials, training videos, exercises, and quizzes are stored permanently on the web hosting and cloud server for continuous online delivery.

Student records

Personal information, enrolments, attendance, progress, assessment results, engagement hours, and Certificate of Attendance records stay on the web hosting while the course is active. Thirty days after course completion they are archived to the institution's on-premises physical server and retained for ten to fifteen years.
