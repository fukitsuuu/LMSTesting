Incident Reporting Manual

Document reference SAF-MAN-014 · revision 3 · 6 pages

1. Purpose

This manual sets out how incidents, near misses, and unsafe conditions are recorded, escalated, and closed. It applies to all employees, contractors, and visitors on company premises.

2. Definitions

An incident is any unplanned event that resulted in injury, illness, or damage. A near miss is an event that could have caused harm but did not. An unsafe condition is a hazard observed before any event occurs.

3. Who must report

Any person who witnesses or is involved in an incident must report it. Reporting is mandatory regardless of severity. Failure to report is treated as a policy breach.

4. Reporting timeline

Verbal notification to the supervisor must occur immediately. The written incident report must be submitted within 24 hours of the event. Reports involving serious injury are escalated to the safety officer within one hour.

5. Required information

Every report must state the date and time, exact location, persons involved, description of what happened, immediate action taken, witnesses, and any equipment involved. Photographs should be attached where it is safe to take them.

6. Investigation and closure

The safety officer classifies the incident, determines root cause, and assigns corrective actions with named owners and target dates. The record is closed only after the corrective action is verified as effective. Closed records are retained for the period defined in the records retention policy.
